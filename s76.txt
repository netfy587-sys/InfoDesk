<!-- WHY -->
<section>
  <div class="wrap two-col">
    <div>
      <h2 class="title">Why the body keeps asking for it</h2>
      <div class="measure">
        <p>Protein is part of every cell. It repairs muscle, builds the structure of skin and hair, forms hormones and enzymes, and keeps the immune system running. Wherever the body rebuilds something, protein supplies the material.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="measure">
      <p>It's made of amino acids — nine of which the body can't produce on its own and has to get from food every day. Plants carry the exact same nine; the difference between sources is proportion, not presence.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
